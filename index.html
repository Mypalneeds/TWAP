About the Book

A republic at the edge
of its own reckoning — the political fiction novel There Was a President.

In the Republic of Valdoria — a nation of ninety million souls built on oil, faith, and the long memory of failed promises — a new president speaks one word that changes everything. Gone.

Told through the lives of those the headlines forget — a market woman doing impossible mathematics by lamplight, a priest walking among the ruins of twenty burned villages, a young man in a cage the law will not open — There Was a President is a novel about what power costs, and who always pays.

Rendered in six devastating parts, M.M.Zyro's debut work is an act of witness dressed as fiction: precise, unsparing, and impossible to look away from.

About There Was a President

There Was a President is a political fiction novel by M.M.Zyro set in the entirely fictional Republic of Valdoria. A new president announces the removal of a fuel subsidy — one word that triggers national economic collapse. The novel follows six storylines: a market woman surviving impossible mathematics, communities massacred without accountability, a political prisoner held without fair trial, a powerful preacher who stays silent, brutal security forces, and a generation that finally rises in protest. It is a story about governance, power, silence, and who always pays the price.

The novel is written by M.M.Zyro, a political fiction author. The book is set in the entirely fictional Republic of Valdoria. All characters, institutions, events, and statistics in the novel are invented. The work draws on universal themes of governance and human dignity rather than depicting any real nation or individual.
